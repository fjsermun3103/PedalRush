ABOUT PEDALRUSH

More Than a Ride.

A Gravity-Driven Culture.

PedalRush is a digital space built around the intensity, style, and identity of mountain biking — from elite downhill racing to freeride expression and global MTB events.

We explore the disciplines that define modern gravity riding: downhill, enduro, freeride, slopestyle, and big-mountain lines shaped by speed, precision, and creativity.

PedalRush is also about the world around the bike: legendary competitions, rider stories, emerging talent, technical progression, and the visual culture that has made MTB one of the most exciting action sports on the planet.
