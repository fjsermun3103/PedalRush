FOUNDATIONS

What Is Mountain Biking?

Mountain biking is a high-intensity outdoor sport built around speed, control, endurance and adaptation across changing terrain.

Riders navigate steep climbs, fast descents, rocky trails and unpredictable conditions, testing both physical ability and mental focus.

From explosive downhill runs to strategic enduro stages and demanding cross-country racing, mountain biking combines athletic performance with technical precision and trail awareness.

Speed

Fast reactions and precise control on changing terrain.

Endurance

Physical resistance across long rides and technical stages.

Skill

Line choice, balance, braking and bike handling under pressure.

More than a sport, mountain biking is a culture driven by freedom, progression and the pursuit of the next great line.

01 Gravity Racing

Downhill

The rawest form of mountain biking.

Riders race against the clock on steep, technical descents, pushing speed and control to the absolute limit. Every line matters, every mistake counts. It is gravity, adrenaline and commitment with no second chances.

Timed race Steep terrain Maximum speed

02 Endurance and Strategy

Enduro

Where endurance meets aggression

Enduro combines long days in the mountains with full-speed timed descents. Riders must manage energy, adapt to natural terrain and stay technically sharp across multiple stages.

Multi-stage Natural trails Maximum speed

Featured event: UCI Enduro World Cup

Event info
03 Creativity and Style

Freeride

Mountain biking as pure expression

Freeride is built around creativity, confidence and line choice. Instead of following a strict race format, riders interpret terrain with drops, jumps and technical features, turning each run into a statement of style.

Expression-based riding Natural or built terrain Style and creativity

Featured event: Red Bull Rampage

Event info
04 Tricks, progression and Show

Slopestyle

Progression in motion

Slopestyle blends technical bike control with aerial creativity. Riders perform tricks over a course filled with jumps, drops and man-made features, where execution, amplitude and style all matter.

Judged run Jump lines Tricks and execution

Featured event: Crankworx Slopestyle

05 Controlled Risk

Big Mountain

Big mountain riding is controlled chaos

Big mountain riding takes place on exposed terrain where riders must read the mountain in real time. Every line demands full commitment, confidence and the ability to stay composed in unpredictable conditions.

Line-based riding Exposed alpine faces Commitment and terrain reading
