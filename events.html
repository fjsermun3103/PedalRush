Main MTB Competitions

From freeride showcases to elite World Cup circuits, these competitions define the energy, progression and identity of modern mountain biking.

Freeride · Utah
Iconic Event

Red Bull Rampage

The most extreme freeride showcase in mountain biking.

Red Bull Rampage is one of the most spectacular freeride MTB events in the world, known for massive natural terrain features, huge drops and highly creative lines carved into the desert cliffs of Utah.

Freeride Natural terrain Judged runs
Known for

Big drops and style

Location

Utah, USA

Legend riders

Brandon Semenuk, Kyle Strait, Kurt Sorge

More than a competition, Rampage represents the most creative and high-consequence side of mountain biking, where progression, line choice and commitment define every run.

The highest level of elite downhill racing.

The UCI Downhill World Cup is the premier international downhill series, where riders race against the clock on steep, technical tracks built around speed, precision and risk management.

Alpine Race · France
Legendary Race

Megavalanche

One of the most iconic downhill marathon events in the Alps.

Megavalanche is famous for its high-altitude mass start and its long descent from snow-covered alpine terrain down to the valley, combining speed, chaos and technical endurance in one unforgettable race.

Mass start Alpine terrain Endurance + gravity
Known for

Chaotic glacier starts

Location

Alpe d'Huez, France

Historic names

Rémy Absalon, René Wildhaber, Hugo Pigeon

Its combination of extreme terrain, unpredictable conditions and huge rider fields has made Megavalanche one of the most recognisable MTB races in Europe.

Festival · World Tour
Global Festival

Crankworx World Tour

The biggest festival and competition platform in mountain biking.

Crankworx blends racing, freestyle and MTB culture through multiple events such as slopestyle, dual slalom, pump track and downhill, hosted at iconic destinations across the world.

Festival format Multi-discipline Global stops
Known for

Slopestyle and MTB culture

Main stops

Whistler, Rotorua, Mont-Sainte-Anne

Standout riders

Emil Johansson and world-class slopestyle athletes

More than a tour, Crankworx has become a meeting point for elite riders, rising talent and fans, celebrating every side of modern mountain biking.

Enduro · World Cup
Elite Series

UCI Enduro World Cup

The top international platform for enduro racing.

The UCI Enduro World Cup represents the elite level of enduro competition, combining timed downhill stages with demanding liaisons across varied natural terrain that test speed, endurance and consistency.

Enduro Multi-stage racing Natural terrain
Known for

Stamina and technical speed

Format

Timed stages + transfers

Historic names

Richie Rude and top global enduro specialists

It continues to push the discipline forward by rewarding riders who can stay fast, composed and technically sharp across an entire race day.
